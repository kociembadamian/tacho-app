# About the app:
[TACHO app](https://tacho.kocie.mba) is a simple and reliable tool for professional drivers, designed for the ongoing monitoring of driving, work and rest time. The app makes daily work on the road easier by automating calculations arising from EU Regulation (EC) No 561/2006 and warning about the risk of exceeding the permitted limits.

The system supports proper planning of the working day, monitoring rest periods, and eliminating errors that occur when keeping activity records manually.

# Key features:
- Activity logging and automatic mode
    Manual event entry or quick switching between states (Driving, Work, Standby, Break) via a Start/Stop button. The app lets you correct the history of manual entries on the fly, making it easier to reflect the actual course of the working day.

- Analysis of regulatory violations (561/2006)
    The algorithm analyses the entered data in real time. The app flags potential breaches of the norms, taking into account, among others, the daily driving limit (9h, with permitted extensions up to 10h), required breaks during continuous driving (4.5h), daily rest period rules (regular and reduced), and weekly and bi-weekly driving time limits.

- Support for crewed driving (Tandem / Double crew)
    A dedicated mode for two drivers present in the vehicle at the same time. It allows efficient logging of activity for both crew members and a seamless switch of roles, with no need to log out or create separate accounts.

- Privacy-first
    All data on your routes, activity and working time is saved and stored exclusively in your phone's memory. The app doesn't require creating an account or sending trip history to external servers. The only information transmitted is anonymous usage statistics and error reports, used to improve the app's stability.

# Who is this app for?
- Truck drivers in domestic and international transport (C, C+E)
- Drivers working in a double crew
- Anyone who needs an additional working-time verifier alongside a digital tachograph
